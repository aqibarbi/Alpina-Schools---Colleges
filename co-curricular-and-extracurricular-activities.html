Co-Curricular and Extracurricular Activities

Education at Alpina goes beyond the classroom. Students explore interests, build leadership skills, and gain confidence through meaningful participation in activities that complement academic learning and develop creativity, communication, teamwork, and initiative.

Opportunities include

  • Debates, quizzes, and public speaking competitions
  • Drama, theatre, and literary societies
  • English, Urdu, and Science societies
  • Student publications and the school magazine
  • Bazm-e-Adab, Hamd, Naat, and Qirat competitions
  • Community service initiatives and leadership programmes

Through these experiences, students learn to express themselves thoughtfully, work well with others, and contribute to their school and the wider community.

  • Recent Honours and Awards
  • Sports and Physical Education
  • Arts, Music and School Band
