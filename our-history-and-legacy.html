Where we began

Alpina was founded in 1983 to provide high-quality education grounded in academic rigour, strong values, and social responsibility.

How we have grown

Today Alpina is a network of 12 campuses and sections in Bahawalpur, Hasilpur, and Lodhran. We teach students from Play Group through GCE O Level, Matriculation, and Intermediate.

More than 30,000 students have been educated and graduated at Alpina. Many have gone on to leading universities and professional careers in Pakistan and abroad.

Our average class size has stayed at 21 students. This supports individual attention and real engagement between teacher and student.

Accreditation and affiliation

Alpina is accredited and affiliated with:

  • Board of Intermediate & Secondary Education (BISE)
  • British Council
  • Aga Khan University Examination Board (AKU-EB)

Our legacy today

Alpina is a leading private educational institution in the region. We aim for a balanced education that combines academic excellence, character development, and a global outlook, while staying true to the values we were founded on.
