Frequently Asked Questions

Admission How to get admission?

Admission has five steps:

  1. Fill in the online form.
  2. Submit the required documents.
  3. Sit an entrance test, if applicable.
  4. Interview with the admission committee.
  5. Receive the decision.

For details, contact the Admission Office.

Admission What is the transfer application process?

Transfer students follow these steps:

  1. Submit previous school records.
  2. Take an assessment test.
  3. Academic standing is reviewed.
  4. Admission is considered based on available seats.

Applications are accepted year-round, subject to availability.

General Why should I attend Alpina college?

Families choose Alpina for:

  • Experienced, qualified faculty
  • Modern facilities and infrastructure
  • A comprehensive curriculum
  • Extracurricular activities
  • Career counseling and guidance
  • A strong alumni network
Academic What are the school timings?

The morning shift runs from 8:00 AM to 1:30 PM. Timings may change during exams and special events, so check the academic calendar.

Facilities Is transportation available?

Yes. Transport covers major city routes. Buses have GPS tracking and trained staff. Transport fees are extra and depend on distance.

Activities What extracurricular activities are offered?

Alpina offers:

  • Sports: cricket, football, basketball, swimming
  • Music and arts
  • Debate and public speaking
  • Science clubs
  • Community service programmes
  • Educational trips
Financial What is the fee structure?

Fees are competitive and transparent. They include tuition, a one-time admission fee, a refundable security deposit, an examination fee, and an extracurricular activity fee.

For exact amounts, contact the accounts office.

Financial Are scholarships available?

Yes. Options include merit-based scholarships (academic excellence and sports), need-based financial aid, and a sibling discount.

Applications are accepted at the start of each academic year.
