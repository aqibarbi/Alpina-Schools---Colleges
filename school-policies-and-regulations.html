School Policies and Regulations

Alpina Schools & Colleges operates under clearly defined policies covering tuition, academics, student conduct, health and safety, communication, and institutional governance. These policies are meant to ensure transparency, fairness, discipline, and the wellbeing of all students.

Parents and students are expected to familiarise themselves with the school’s policies. They are part of enrolment and continued attendance.
