The School Insignia and Identity

The school insignia

The name Alpina comes from the Alpine mountains, regarded as symbols of grandeur, endurance, and quiet strength. We want to instil these values in our students: intellectual ambition, resilience, and dignity of purpose.

  • Inverted triangles

    At the base, they represent the enduring pursuit of knowledge and a commitment to lifelong learning.

  • Ascending peaks

    They symbolise the shared journey of the school, its students, and their families toward academic achievement and personal excellence.

The insignia reflects relationships built on trust, shared values, and a common vision for the future.

A symbol of partnership and purpose

The insignia reminds us of our commitment to work with parents, students, and the wider community. Through this partnership we aim to nurture curiosity, character, and competence. Our reputation, built over more than four decades, reflects integrity, academic rigour, and thoughtful leadership.

Our motto

Learning Is The Greatest Conquest.

The motto reflects our belief that pursuing knowledge is the most meaningful and enduring achievement. It empowers individuals, strengthens communities, and shapes a better future.
