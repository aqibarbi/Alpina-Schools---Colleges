Arts, Music and School Band

Alpina puts strong emphasis on artistic expression as a vital part of student development. The arts give students a structured, supportive setting to explore creativity, discipline, and emotional expression.

Music and school band

Alpina is among the few private institutions in the region to keep a dedicated School Band. Trained instructors supervise the band, which builds musical literacy, coordination, teamwork, and performance discipline. Students regularly represent the school at competitions and official events.

Visual and expressive arts

Students take part in art, music, creative writing, and performance-based activities that build imagination and confidence. Drama, theatre, and cultural presentations develop communication skills and self-expression.

We cultivate creativity alongside academic rigour, to ensure balanced personal development.
