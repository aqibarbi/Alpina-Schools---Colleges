School Books and Uniform Guidelines

School books

The prescribed syllabus and textbooks are sold through approved booksellers. Contact the School Office for current authorised vendors, booklists, and purchasing details.

School uniform

We want a neat, dignified uniform appearance that reflects discipline, equality, and belonging. Parents must make sure students wear the prescribed uniform at all times. The School Office can give you the latest uniform specifications and authorised suppliers.

Girls’ uniform

Season and class Items
Summer, Play Group to Class 3 Prescribed school uniform; black shoes with flat heels
Summer, Class 4 onwards Blue check shirt; white shalwar with white dupatta; white scarf
Winter Same as summer, plus red cardigan and navy blue blazer

Given the school’s Islamic profile, heads must be covered while entering and leaving the school premises.

Boys’ uniform

Season and class Items
Summer, Play Group to Class 3 Blue check shirt; navy blue shorts; white socks; black shoes
Summer, Class 4 onwards Blue check shirt; navy blue trousers
Winter White shirt; grey trousers; grey sweater; navy blue blazer; maroon tie

Physical training (P.T.) uniform

The P.T. uniform applies in summer and winter, and is worn only on designated P.T. days.

Group Items
Girls, Class 1 to 5 School tracksuit; white socks or leggings; white tennis shoes
Girls, Class 6 onwards White shirt; white shalwar and dupatta; white joggers or sports shoes with socks. A school tracksuit with dupatta is also permitted.
Boys, Class 1 to Class 8 / 10 School tracksuit; white socks; white tennis shoes or joggers

General guidelines

  • All blazers must have the school badge securely sewn onto the pocket.
  • Sweaters, blazers, lunch boxes, water bottles, and personal items must be clearly labelled.
  • Uniform regulations must be strictly observed.
  • Jewellery, make-up, nail polish, and accessories are not permitted. A wristwatch is allowed.
  • Girls with long hair must keep it neatly tied.
  • Boys must keep a neat haircut and grooming.
  • Students must not bring valuables, mobile phones, or electronic devices to school.
  • Personal hygiene is monitored regularly, and parents will be informed if concerns arise.
