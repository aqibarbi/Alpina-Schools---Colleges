Digital Learning and Information Technology

Technology is integrated purposefully as a core part of teaching and learning at Alpina. Our IT infrastructure gives students age-appropriate access to digital tools, to build confidence, competence, and responsible digital citizenship from an early age.

  • Junior classes get structured computer access within the curriculum.
  • Senior students get one-to-one access, which supports research, coursework, and project-based learning.
  • Our modern Computer Labs and IT Resource Centres have over 150 networked computers, and a wide range of curriculum-aligned educational software.
  • Access is supervised, including internet and email use, to balance productivity with online safety.

Digital learning goes beyond basic computer literacy. Students are systematically introduced to essential software applications, research skills, and practical problem-solving tools. The goal is to prepare learners for the academic, professional, and ethical demands of an increasingly digital world.
