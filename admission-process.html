Admission Process

Admission follows a structured, transparent process designed to give each child the best possible placement. It has four stages.

  • 1. Application

    Parents visit the campus with the child and complete the Admission Application Form. The form is issued with the school prospectus at registration.

  • 2. Academic assessment

    The child takes an age-appropriate placement assessment. For Play Group and Nursery (ages 3–4) it is mainly oral and observational, focusing on readiness, communication, and social interaction. For higher classes it assesses readiness for the intended class.

  • 3. Parent and student interview

    We meet the parent(s) and the child, to understand the child’s educational needs and check alignment with our academic culture, expectations, and values.

  • 4. Admission confirmation

    If admission is approved, parents submit the prescribed documents and pay the admission fee and a refundable security deposit. Admission is confirmed once all formalities are complete.

Selection and placement

Each year we receive a significantly higher number of applications than available places. Decisions are based on a holistic evaluation of the child’s academic readiness, abilities and skills, and overall potential to benefit from the Alpina learning environment.
