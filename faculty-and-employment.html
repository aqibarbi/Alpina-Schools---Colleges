Faculty and Employment

Alpina Schools & Colleges welcomes applications from qualified, motivated, and committed educators who share our values of academic excellence, discipline, and professional integrity. Faculty recruitment is conducted through our campuses, so that new colleagues match our academic standards and institutional culture.

Applying in person

Visit the relevant campus during office hours, and bring:

  • An updated Curriculum Vitae (CV)
  • Copies of your academic and professional qualifications
  • A copy of your CNIC

The academic leadership team reviews applications, and shortlisted candidates are contacted.

Faculty recruitment assistance

For guidance on teaching vacancies and eligibility criteria, contact the Faculty Recruitment Desk on WhatsApp: +92 305 5504444.

Alpina is an equal opportunity institution, committed to professional growth, academic excellence, and ethical education.
