Message from the Directors

In their message, the Founding Directors and Directors of Alpina Schools & Colleges set out how they see the purpose of the school.

The union of knowledge and character.

The purpose of education

The Directors see education as more than teaching academic subjects. It is about shaping character, intellect, and purpose. The school aims to help students reach their full potential and contribute to society through a well-rounded education. This guides the curriculum, teaching, student support, and community engagement.

Knowledge and character

They argue that excellence comes from combining knowledge with character. Integrity, compassion, and moral clarity help students act responsibly. The goal is critical thinkers who act ethically and lead with humility.

Academic programmes

The school is affiliated with BISE, GCE O Level, and AKU-EB Matriculation. Its curriculum combines technology, thematic learning, character education, and project-based work. Students receive focused teaching and individual attention, and families are meant to feel supported.

Early years

From Pre-Primary through Kindergarten, a Montessori-influenced, technology-supported setting builds curiosity, independence, and a love of learning. Across all levels, the curriculum links subjects together. Students also study Nazirah, Tafseer, and Arabic for spiritual and moral development.

People and results

The Directors credit experienced faculty, modern facilities, and an active parent community for high standards. Students regularly do well in Board exams and go on to universities in Pakistan and abroad.

Older students

As students advance, the school emphasises independence and maturity. Structured timetables, student services, and career and study-abroad counselling help them plan their futures. The libraries hold university prospectuses from UK and US institutions.

Alumni

The school has more than 30,000 graduates, known for professional success, leadership, and social responsibility. The Directors see them as evidence of the school’s values.

An invitation

The Directors thank readers for considering the school and invite them to meet the admissions team to learn about programmes and campus life. They hope to help shape confident, capable, and principled individuals who are ready for the future.
