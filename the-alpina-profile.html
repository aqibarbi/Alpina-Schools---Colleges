The Alpina Profile

A comprehensive educational experience grounded in academic excellence, character development, and balanced growth.

Early years and foundations

  • A pioneer in pre-primary education, including a Play Group for three-year-olds.
  • Strong emphasis on early literacy, numeracy, and social development.
  • Nazira classes and structured Islamic education from the early years.

Academic excellence

  • Consistently strong academic outcomes, including top positions in BISE.
  • GCE O Level, Matriculation, and Intermediate programmes.
  • A rigorous, balanced, and continuously evolving curriculum.
  • Students complete the Holy Qur’an by Class 5, with the grace of Allah.

Faculty and leadership

  • Highly qualified, skilled, and dedicated teaching faculty.
  • In-house professional development led by experienced educators.
  • Leadership with international exposure and deep institutional experience.

Learning resources and facilities

  • An extensive central school library and classroom libraries.
  • Fully equipped science laboratories across levels.
  • Modern computer laboratories with supervised internet access.
  • Technology and digital skills integrated from Play Group onwards.

Student life and enrichment

  • Separate campuses for boys and girls.
  • Debates, drama, quizzes, projects, competitions, and societies.
  • School band, cultural events, and community service initiatives.
  • Educational excursions and trips with full staff supervision.
  • Structured sports programmes and playground facilities.

Guidance and beyond the classroom

  • Career guidance and study-abroad counselling.
  • Emphasis on personal responsibility, leadership, and character.
  • A school magazine and digital platforms that show student work and school life.

Affiliations and recognition

  • Affiliated with BISE, AKU-EB, and the British Council.
  • Member of the Central Bureau for International Education & Training.
