Expeditions, Trips and Picnics

Educational experiences at Alpina extend beyond the campus through carefully planned excursions, trips, and recreational outings. They enrich classroom learning, broaden perspectives, and strengthen social bonds among students.

Each class takes part in

  • An annual picnic, organised with full staff supervision
  • Educational visits to institutions, exhibitions, and culturally significant locations, where appropriate

All trips are planned with an emphasis on safety, purpose, and age-appropriate learning outcomes. They encourage curiosity, independence, and real-world understanding, and create lasting memories for students.
