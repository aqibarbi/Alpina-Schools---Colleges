Pre-Primary Programme

Our Pre-Primary Programme lays the foundation for a lifetime of learning. It supports early cognitive, social, emotional, and moral development in a structured, nurturing setting.

Levels

  • Play Group
  • Nursery
  • Kindergarten

What children learn

The core focus is early literacy, numeracy, communication skills, and social interaction, alongside age-appropriate play, exploration, and routine. Learning experiences are planned to build curiosity, confidence, and positive learning habits.

  • Language development
  • Fine and gross motor skills
  • Early problem-solving
  • Classroom routines, listening skills, cooperation, and self-expression

Teaching is guided, supportive, and responsive, to help children move into formal schooling.

Values and faith

Moral values, manners, and social responsibility are woven into daily activities. Nazira and basic Islamic learning are introduced gently and in a structured way, encouraging respect, discipline, and spiritual awareness.

Small classes, close communication

Small class sizes, trained early-years educators, and close communication with parents give each child individual attention. Parents are invited to visit our Pre-Primary campuses.
